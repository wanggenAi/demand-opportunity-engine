# Brain policy V4 — MANY VALIDATIONS, FEW HEAVY COMMITMENTS

Human-authorized correction, 2026-10-14. This supersedes old single-primary-bet restrictions in README, bootstrap and historical sprint briefs. It changes daily allocation, not the business model or sales truth gates.

One human can run many small bets, but only a few heavy commitments. DIRECT_LEAD, PRODUCT_SIGNAL and INFO_EDGE remain independent and refresh together. Do not pause two routes while pursuing one.

- DIRECT: qualified CONTACT_TODAY <=5; real due FOLLOW_UP <=5; bounded desk VALIDATE_FIRST; stale/high-competition/incapable records SKIP.
- PRODUCT: validation <=5, presell/customer interviews <=3, active BUILD <=2. Watch multiple hypotheses. No high score grants a build: check independent and cross-platform paying demand, current alternatives/prices, standardization, distribution, controlled delivery and a real willing tester/preorder.
- INFO: WATCH can cover 10–30 windows, validation <=5, EXPLOIT <=3. Exact event date, primary verification, buyer-awareness gap, economic value, decay, competition, monetization and legality remain mandatory; no measured awareness gap means no exploitation.
- Total validations <=15; open quotes <=5; heavy deliveries <=3; WATCH may exceed 50. These are upper limits, never targets to fill.

The daily artifact is DAILY_MONEY_PORTFOLIO.md (also TODAY_MONEY_BOARD.md), with separate CONTACT_TODAY, FOLLOW_UP, PRODUCT VALIDATIONS, INFO EDGE VALIDATIONS, ACTIVE BUILDS, ACTIVE DELIVERY and TODAY ACTION PLAN sections. JSON preserves full skip/watch records and allocation inputs.

Actual recorded human time supports revenue/hour; planning estimates never become outcome facts.
