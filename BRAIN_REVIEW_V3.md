# Brain Review V3 — buyer intent found, source-market fit is now the bottleneck

## Verdict

Third sprint passes its core objective: the engine can now distinguish explicit commercial buyers from pain observers and sellers, preserve buyer-authored commercial evidence, and refuse to promote high-competition or stale requests into `DO NOW`.

This is meaningful progress. The remaining problem is no longer scoring correctness. It is **source-market fit**.

The latest run found 7 verified commercial buyers, but `DO NOW = 0`. The strongest buyer source was the n8n Jobs community, where visible provider competition ranged from roughly a dozen applicants to more than one hundred. That is a real market, but not yet a favorable market for this operator.

## Strategic correction

Do not loosen the gates to manufacture opportunities.

Instead, search where the operator has a structural advantage:

1. **China-local execution and research**
   - China supplier/factory research
   - supplier verification and background research
   - Chinese-language RFQ preparation and communication
   - 1688 / Alibaba / manufacturer research
   - overseas buyer needing a China-side coordinator or researcher
   - technical product sourcing / vendor comparison
   - China market or competitor research

2. **Remote technical work with bounded scope**
   - automation diagnostics
   - workflow reliability / QA
   - data research / lead research
   - competitor monitoring
   - API / integration troubleshooting
   - implementation work where evidence of fit can be demonstrated honestly

3. **Repeatable paid pains for productization**
   - only after repeated independent buyers/payment evidence cluster around the same narrow job

4. **Current lawful information edges**
   - only when a real target buyer group, awareness gap and monetization path are evidenced

## Why this matters

A crowded buyer post is not automatically a good lead. The ideal lead is not merely `PAID`; it is:

`explicit buyer intent × freshness × reachability × low/medium competition × operator advantage × bounded deliverability`.

The next sprint should optimize discovery toward that intersection.

## Hard principles for the next round

- Do not reduce competition penalties just to produce `DO NOW`.
- Do not claim factory inspection, freight, DDP, legal compliance or specialist services that are not controlled.
- Search engines may be used for discovery, but a lead cannot be promoted without resolving to a primary/original source with buyer-authored evidence and a legitimate contact/application path.
- One forum/category must not dominate the apparent market. Measure source yield and diversify based on results.
- A source that produces many records but few fresh, low-competition, explicit buyers is low-value noise.
- Keep GitHub/HN primarily as PRODUCT_SIGNAL/INFO_EDGE inputs unless an item contains genuine buyer-authored commercial intent.

## Success definition

The fourth sprint is successful only if it either:

1. finds at least one defensible `DO NOW` buyer with a lawful contact path and a credible operator advantage, **or**
2. proves with source-yield data that the attempted markets do not currently produce such leads and identifies the next source family to test.

Code completeness alone is not success.
