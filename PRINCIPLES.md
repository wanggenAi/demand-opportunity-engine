# Operating Principles

1. **Revenue before architecture.**
2. **No building without demand evidence.**
3. **Conversation beats code.** If 20 prospects can be contacted faster than a feature can be built, contact them first.
4. **Evidence hierarchy:** payment/job post > explicit request > repeated complaint > search intent > engagement > AI speculation.
5. **One human, one active money problem.** System may monitor thousands; human actively pursues one primary bet and one small experiment.
6. **System parallel, human serial.** Three opportunity routes run in parallel; human attention is ranked globally.
7. **Legal, ethical, durable information arbitrage only.** No stolen accounts, fraud, unauthorized access, payment abuse, circumvention, or deceptive resale.
8. **Every opportunity must have a next action.** If there is no reachable buyer or validation step, it is not actionable.
9. **Decay matters.** Information edges lose value over time and require faster validation.
10. **Feedback closes the loop.** Every contacted opportunity records reply / no reply / quote / win / loss / revenue.

## Decision rule
Every engineering task must answer one of these questions:
- Does it help us find a buyer faster?
- Does it help us judge willingness to pay more accurately?
- Does it help us contact or convert a buyer?
- Does it help us identify a repeatable product opportunity?
- Does it help us identify and exploit a legal information edge before it decays?
- Does it improve learning from actual market outcomes?

If the answer is no, deprioritize it.
