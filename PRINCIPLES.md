# Operating Principles

## ZERO-COST ACQUISITION RULE — current highest-priority commercial constraint

Current acquisition budget = 0. Only evidenced `acquisition_cost = ZERO` can enter CONTACT_TODAY / DO NOW. Values are ZERO / OPTIONAL_PAID / REQUIRED_PAID / UNKNOWN; missing evidence defaults to UNKNOWN. A free contact channel still requires lawful account/forum eligibility.

Do not buy Connects, bids, memberships, proposal boosts, job credits, contact unlocks, SEO APIs or lead databases, or recharge to seek customers. REQUIRED_PAID is BLOCKED_BY_PAID_ACQUISITION. Existing genuinely free credits are occasional exceptions only; such platforms are not core acquisition channels. Upwork may supply market intelligence but is removed from the primary execution test.

The previous ten-request zero-cost and r/forhire tests are complete and stopped. Their scan bounds remain historical audit limits, not a single-contact daily policy. Future discovery stays bounded to configured source budgets. Buyer-posted email, invited DM and permitted free replies/applications take priority. Ordinary pain, seller ads and bounty inquiries are not paid buyers.

## Standing principles

1. **Revenue before architecture.**
2. **No building without demand evidence.**
3. **Conversation beats code.** If 20 prospects can be contacted faster than a feature can be built, contact them first.
4. **Evidence hierarchy:** payment/job post > explicit request > repeated complaint > search intent > engagement > AI speculation.
5. **MANY VALIDATIONS, FEW HEAVY COMMITMENTS.** One human can run many small bets, but only a few heavy commitments.
6. **Three routes and small bets run in parallel.** Rank the daily combination by value/conversion, speed to cash, structural advantage and repeatability per human time, cash cost, delivery risk and competition. Unknown financial inputs remain unknown, with neutral allocation factors.
7. **Legal, ethical, durable information arbitrage only.** No stolen accounts, fraud, unauthorized access, payment abuse, circumvention, or deceptive resale.
8. **Every opportunity must have a next action.** If there is no reachable buyer or validation step, it is not actionable.
9. **Decay matters.** Information edges lose value over time and require faster validation.
10. **Feedback closes the loop.** Every contacted opportunity records reply / no reply / quote / win / loss / revenue.

## Decision rule
Every engineering task must answer one of these questions:
- Does it help us find a buyer faster?
- Does it help us judge willingness to pay more accurately?
- Does it help us contact or convert a buyer?
- Does it help us identify a repeatable product opportunity?
- Does it help us identify and exploit a legal information edge before it decays?
- Does it improve learning from actual market outcomes?

If the answer is no, deprioritize it.

## Portfolio WIP defaults

CONTACT_TODAY <=5/day; FOLLOW_UP <=5/day; open QUOTED <=5; all active validations <=15 (typically 10–15); product validations <=5, presell/customer interviews <=3; info validations <=5 (EXPLOIT <=3); active BUILD <=2; heavy DELIVERY <=3. WATCH is not an active commitment and can exceed 50. These are caps, not quotas. Do not hide a real WIP overflow; pause new commitments until it is resolved.

CONTACT_TODAY requires the unchanged buyer/payment/freshness/zero-cost/competition/capability gates. VALIDATE_FIRST permits desk checks only until these pass. High competition, obsolete demand and capability mismatches belong in SKIP, not endless validation. FOLLOW_UP requires real CONTACTED and an unfinished, due conversation. BUILD requires repeated independent cross-platform paying demand, verified competitor prices, standardized delivery, distribution, low cost, controlled dependencies and a primary willing tester/preorder. A high score alone never authorizes BUILD. Active delivery requires real WON and human-reported active delivery, not a prediction.

Proposed prices are not buyer budgets. Portfolio priority is an allocation index, not a revenue promise. No history means neutral assumptions; real source/cluster/offer reply, quote, win, realized ticket, cycle and human-time revenue metrics refine future allocation. Record actual human_minutes in outcome events only when supplied by the human.
